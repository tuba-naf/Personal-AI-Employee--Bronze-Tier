# Company Handbook - Bronze Tier

## Mission
Provide a steady stream of verified, factual content drafts for LinkedIn, Instagram, and News platforms. **All content is strictly focused on sustainability, climate change, and environmental topics** — with Pakistan relevance and a Problem-to-Hope narrative arc.

## Rules of Engagement

### Content Rules
1. **AI only drafts content** — no posting, no automation beyond drafting
2. All drafts must be **verified and approved by humans** before any use
3. Completed drafts are moved to `/Completed/`
4. Watchers create Plans for new tasks automatically
5. All content must be **factual, verified, and include real case studies, statistics, and references**

### Content Focus Areas
All content must be about one or more of these themes:
- **Climate Change** — global warming, emissions, IPCC reports, COP summits, net zero
- **Sustainability** — renewable energy, circular economy, green technology, sustainable development
- **Environment** — pollution (air, water, plastic), deforestation, biodiversity loss, conservation
- **Natural Disasters** — floods, droughts, heat waves, glacial melt (climate-linked)
- **Green Solutions** — solar/wind energy, reforestation, waste management, clean water, EVs

### Content Rotation Pattern
Follow this cycle continuously for each platform:
1. **Local Problem Post** — Highlight a current environmental or climate crisis in Pakistan with facts, figures, case studies, and credible references
2. **Local Hopeful Post** — Show green solutions, sustainability success stories, or environmental initiatives in Pakistan
3. **Global Problem Post** — Highlight a trending global climate/environmental crisis, then connect its relevance or lessons for Pakistan
4. **Global Hopeful Post** — Showcase global green innovations, climate solutions, or sustainability breakthroughs, and how they could inspire environmental action in Pakistan

### Content Standards
- LinkedIn posts: 500+ words
- Instagram captions: 300+ words
- News drafts: Include critique of policies/practices + hopeful angle
- All content must include real-life examples and verifiable data
- Only content from the last 7–14 days should be considered for relevance

### Draft Delivery Schedule
- **LinkedIn & Instagram:** Drafts prepared every 2 days
- **News Drafts:** Drafts prepared daily

### Workflow
1. Watchers detect trending topics or content opportunities
2. Draft `.md` files are created in `/Needs_Action/`
3. AI or human verifies facts, sources, and figures
4. Human reviews for tone, clarity, and accuracy
5. Approved drafts moved to `/Completed/`
6. Dashboard.md is updated to reflect current state

## Security & Privacy
- No credentials required at Bronze Tier
- All content creation occurs locally in the Obsidian vault
- No external posting or API calls for publishing
- Keep local copies of all drafts

## Folder Reference
| Folder | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| `/Inbox` | Raw task drops and inputs |
| `/Needs_Action` | Drafts awaiting verification and review |
| `/Plans` | AI-generated task plans |
| `/Completed` | Verified and approved content |
| `/Watchers` | Watcher scripts per platform |
| `/Logs` | Action and audit logs |
